## What taking part means

We record your voice, which may include background sound, and store transcripts, questions, interview timings,
AI marks, reasons and feedback where relevant to the selected activity. A human marker may also record marks and
comments. Your voice or answer text can identify you even when we use a volunteer ID. Please avoid saying unrelated personal information or other people's details.

We use this material to test the system, measure speech recognition and compare AI and human marks. The project
team, authorized faculty and human marker may review it. MSOT reviewers may receive agreed private submission
evidence. Public release of your recording or identifiable transcript/marks needs your separate optional permission below.

Taking part is voluntary and does not affect your grades. You can decline, stop at any time, or contact Tejas
privately with your volunteer ID to request deletion or withdraw permission for future public use. Public copies
already downloaded or shared by other people may not be recoverable; we will explain the limits of deleting copies
we do not control.

The project source-code register contains only your volunteer ID, consent-signing date and public-demo yes/no choice.
Past versions may remain in Git history. Your name/signature and private identity mapping are kept outside Git.

The proposed retention period is **one calendar month after the recording**, pending MSOT confirmation. The
coordinator writes the due date above and arranges manual removal of recordings, transcripts, marks, signed forms
and related private copies for this session, or earlier removal on request. The prototype does not delete them automatically.
If the retention period, storage recipients or processors change, explain the change and obtain consent again before new use.
