- **What the numbers say:** the speech stack alone is about 0.4-0.55 s. The tail is the LLM: turns where the model phrases a follow-up took 2.7-4.8 s (classify + phrase on Ollama, then Piper on text that is not cached); turns that only classify and ask a pre-rendered bank question took 0.8-1.1 s. The engine does not stream the phrase into TTS yet (T3 measured that lever at about -0.8 s p95).
- **Corrections to earlier numbers (T3/T3b):**
  1. The spike's whisper.cpp timings were *inference only*; each real call also pays process start and model load. Measured on the 22.8 s clip (base q5_1): 0.55 s wall vs 0.425 s inference, so ≈ 0.12 s more per STT call than the T3 tables say. The in-server STT figures above include it.
  2. T3's Piper figure (0.09 s) was the library loaded *in our process*. As a separate process per sentence it is **0.82 s** (start + load). A persistent Piper process fed one line per sentence (what `PiperTTS` does) gives 0.11-0.23 s per sentence after a 0.96 s first call, which keeps the GPL isolation of D19 at a small cost.
- Not verified / not run: **Groq (the intended live LLM) end to end** (≈ 55 live calls; ask-first, the cap applies); real people's speech (all clips are synthetic macOS voices; the 7 real clips are not wired into the bot); hands-free with real VAD (V3: Silero, pre-roll so the first word is not clipped); a browser/microphone; concurrency (one student at a time; Piper pool and whisper spawn at 100+ sessions unmeasured); Hindi/Hinglish through the server (`WHISPER_LANG` exists, untested here); a full 15-minute viva with real timing.
- Risks found:
  - **Plan B trigger:** p95 4.15 s with Ollama is above 4 s. It is the *fallback* LLM; the live path (Groq, 0.45 s per call in T3b) should be well below, but that is unmeasured in this pipeline.
  - Whisper base hallucinated on one synthetic Piper sentence ("A hash table maps..." became "#table maps...") while the next sentences were fine; it is one data point, but it says short first words can be lost. Needs the real-clip run.
  - I committed `.local/` log and code files by mistake in an earlier commit (it went into `main` with PR #2): untracked again in this branch and in the CORE branch; the codes were for a throwaway local database. History still contains them.
  - `whisper-cli` is spawned per answer: fine for one student, a measurable cost at scale (see Suggestions).
- Suggestions:
  1. **Stream the phrase into TTS** (first sentence as soon as it is complete): the biggest lever on the tail; a CORE+VOICE change in `Interviewer`/`StudentService`.
  2. **Approve ~60 live Groq calls** so this exact measurement is repeated with the intended live model (and Gemini Flash-Lite as the second chain entry).
  3. Replace the per-call `whisper-cli` with a persistent `whisper-server` (-0.1 s per answer, less CPU spawn at scale).
  4. D19's numbers should carry a note: STT excluded process start, Piper was in-process, and this pipeline's first real p95 is 4.15 s on the fallback LLM.
  5. Wire the 7 real clips (kept out of git) into the bot as an opt-in dataset to get a real-voice latency/WER check through the server.
