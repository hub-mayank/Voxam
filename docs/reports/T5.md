The scorer chain must start with Gemini flash-lite (see Suggestions). When every provider is out the worker defers (never fails) and polls every 5 minutes; a long outage shows up only as sessions staying `scoring`.
  - The budget **reserves** prompt + `max_tokens` + the adapter's headroom (1,000 for OpenAI-compatible, 1,500 for Gemini) before a call, then corrects it after the reply. A live classify call is about 1,350 reserved against about 400 used, so under many simultaneous students the reservation can make a call wait up to 5 s or fall through to the next provider earlier than the real usage justifies. Tune after T1b measures real tokens.
  - A scoring job holds a 5-minute lease; three runs on a slow chain (60 s timeouts, retries, fallbacks) could exceed it. The slow worker then writes nothing (`lost`) and its calls are wasted. Not seen, not measured.
  - The deferral relies on the scorer wrapping `CallCapExceeded` with `raise ... from e` (`__cause__`); a refactor that drops the chaining would turn deferrals into failures (a test covers it).
