- Evidence (`python scripts/cost_model.py`, sidecars `provider=live` meaning tokens measured live, arithmetic only):
  - **Rupees per viva** (11 answers, 3 scorer runs; interview tokens mean 260 in / 31 out per classify and 162 / 59 per phrase, 0.975 classify and 0.5 phrase calls per answer; scorer 1,223 in / 921 out per call):
    - all local (Ollama + whisper.cpp + Piper): **₹0** marginal (D15; hardware and electricity not included)
    - Groq 20b interview + Groq 20b scorer: **₹0.15** (₹52.6 for 350)
    - Groq 20b interview + Groq 120b scorer: ₹0.26 (₹89.4)
    - Groq 20b interview + Gemini 2.5 flash-lite scorer: ₹0.19 (₹64.9)
    - Groq 20b interview + Gemini 3.1 flash-lite scorer: ₹0.53 (₹184.6)
    - cloud STT (Groq whisper-large-v3-turbo, 15 min of audio) adds ₹0.95 per viva (₹333.6 for 350): **speech recognition would be the largest cost, not the LLM**.
  - **Capacity for 150 at once** (`capacity_plan.csv`): 165.9 interview LLM calls/min and about 44,300 tokens/min against 27 requests/min and 7,200 tokens/min per free Groq model -> **7 model keys** for either limit; scoring everyone together is 964,887 tokens = **5.4 days of one Groq model's daily cap**; one machine decodes about 4.6 answers/s of STT (modelled curve, 7 s answers assumed) against 1.88 needed, and Piper makes at most 4.83 sentences/s.
